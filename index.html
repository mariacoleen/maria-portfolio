About

Meet Maria Coleen.

I'm Maria Coleen Flores, a Psychology student with a growing passion for creative digital work.

I first discovered my love for design and content creation through school programs and my role as a youth leader in our community. Since then, I've gained hands-on experience in social media management, digital marketing, video editing, and graphic design.

With a background in psychology, I bring a deeper understanding of audience behavior and engagement, helping me create content that's both strategic and meaningful. I've managed social media pages, created engaging graphics, edited promotional videos, and built content calendars that align with brand goals.

I combine creativity with clear communication and always strive for quality in every project.
